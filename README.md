## 🏛️ Platform Features & Architecture

### 📰 Wikipedia-Standard Article Layout
Every dispatch and article on 616 Intel follows a refined, distraction-free Wikipedia reading structure:
- **Clean Article Headers**: Bold display titles paired with descriptive hatnotes for context.
- **Structured Table of Contents**: Jump directly to specific sections, analysis angles, or source lists.
- **Hierarchical Content Breakdown**: Distinct sections with crisp horizontal separating rules.
- **Inline Wikilinks**: Seamless blue cross-references linking related characters, teams, movies, and leaks across the entire 340+ article library.
- **Verified References**: Transparent citations referencing industry trade papers, casting sheets, and production ledgers.
- **Zero Distractions**: Strictly no infobox clutter, no intrusive widgets, and no social share bloat.

### ✍️ The Fan Intel Wire (`/write`)
Marvel lore is fueled by its community. 616 Intel features a dedicated open dispatch portal at `/write` where fans, set observers, and comic historians can submit scoops:
- **Instant Contribution**: No user registration, social login, or password required.
- **Text-Only Privacy Policy**: To protect anonymous sources and keep the platform ultra-fast, file and media uploads are completely disabled—pure text reporting with Markdown support.
- **Verification Tiers**: Submissions can be categorized under:
  - `RUMORED` — Unconfirmed casting whispers or word-of-mouth reports
  - `UNVERIFIED` — Clandestine soundstage or social leaks
  - `REPORTED` — Trade publication or production ledger filings
  - `THEORY` — Analytical speculation based on comic continuity
  - `CONFIRMED` — Studio announcements or official statements
- **Real-Time Wikipedia Preview**: Live layout rendering before publication.

## 🧭 Intelligence Verification Standards

At 616 Intel, rumor tracking is treated with journalistic discipline:

| Verification Level | Definition | Source Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CONFIRMED** | Fully corroborated fact | Official studio press release, trade confirmation (Deadline/THR/Variety), or on-record executive interview |
| **REPORTED** | High-credibility industry intel | Production Weekly listings, copyright registry filings, or reputable industry scooper consensus |
| **RUMORED** | Developing whisper | Soundstage crew reports, prop department leaks, or insider message board leaks |
| **THEORY** | Critical speculation | Detailed synthesis of narrative breadcrumbs, comic book parallels, and timeline mechanics |
| **DEBUNKED** | Disproven claim | Explicitly refuted by studio heads, cast members, or photographic proof of alternate production plans |
