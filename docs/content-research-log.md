## 1. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY & SOURCE TAXONOMY

All intelligence ingested into the 616 Intel platform is categorized under a strict 4-tier source taxonomy to preserve journalistic integrity:

1. **PRIMARY / OFFICIAL (`OFFICIAL`, `VIDEO`)**:
   - Direct releases from Marvel Studios, The Walt Disney Company, Sony Pictures Entertainment, official San Diego Comic-Con (SDCC) Hall H presentations, and D23 Expo panels.
   - Official press releases, studio teasers, trade registration documents, and verified YouTube trailer channels (`marvel`, `SonyPictures`, `DisneyPlus`).

2. **SECONDARY / TRADE JOURNALISM (`TRADE`, `NEWS`, `INTERVIEW`)**:
   - Credible entertainment industry trades: *Variety*, *The Hollywood Reporter (THR)*, *Deadline Hollywood*, *The Wrap*, *Entertainment Weekly*, *Empire*, and *IGN*.
   - Direct quotes from filmmakers (Kevin Feige, Anthony & Joe Russo, Matt Shakman, Destin Daniel Cretton), screenwriters (Michael Lesslie, Eric Pearson), and cast members.

3. **TERTIARY / INSIDER & RUMOR TRACKING (`RUMOR`, `LEAKER`, `REPORT`)**:
   - Established scoop reporters and investigative publications: *The Cosmic Circus (Alex Perez)*, *DanielRPK*, *MyTimeToShineHello*, *CanWeGetSomeToast*, *ComicBookMovie*.
   - Rigorously flagged with explicit `claim`, `evidence`, `counterEvidence`, and `editorialNote` fields. Never upgraded to `CONFIRMED` without studio verification.

4. **PHYSICAL EVIDENCE / LEAK INVESTIGATION (`LEAK`, `PHOTO`)**:
   - Raw visual captures, CinemaCon sizzle reel audio/video recordings, call sheet leaks, drone set surveillance, and retail packaging disclosures.
   - Handled under strict ethical guidelines: **Zero piracy links, zero torrent seeds, zero doxxing of background personnel**. Described purely through investigative analysis.
