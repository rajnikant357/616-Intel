## The Worthiness Dilemma: Can Doom Lift Thor's Hammer?

The enchantment placed on Mjolnir by the All-Father Odin states: *"Whosoever holds this hammer, if he be worthy, shall possess the power of Thor."*

Does Doctor Victor von Doom possess the worthiness required to lift Mjolnir?

### The Canonical Test: Fantastic Four #536–537
Following the death of Thor during Ragnarok, Mjolnir fell through the dimensions into the nether realms of Hell, carving an escape conduit for Doctor Doom. Doom battled legions of demons to claim the hammer, believing that his royal lineage and noble intentions to protect humanity made him worthy.

When Doom gripped the uru handle and pulled with all his armored might, **the hammer did not move a single millimeter**. Realizing he was unworthy in the eyes of Odin's magic, Doom abandoned it in disgust.

### Why Doom is Unworthy:
- **Absolute Hubris:** Doom believes he is superior to gods and men, lacking the self-sacrificing humility that Odin’s enchantment demands.
- **Machiavellian Malice:** Doom is willing to murder innocent citizens and sacrifice allies to achieve Latverian objectives.
