## The Cross-Universe War: Can Doomsday Beat the Avengers?

In comic book history, few monsters strike more terror into heroes than **Doomsday**—the prehistoric Kryptonian bio-weapon created by Bertron who beat Superman to death in the streets of Metropolis (*Superman* #75).

Equipped with **reactive evolution**, bottomless stamina, invulnerable bone plating, and the terrifying ability to resurrect immune to whatever previously killed him, Doomsday is a walking extinction-level event.

If Doomsday crash-landed on Earth-616 outside Avengers Compound, could Earth’s Mightiest Heroes survive? **The answer depends entirely on which division of the Avengers takes the field.**

---

## 1. Wave 1: The Ground & Tech Avengers (Total Slaughter)

If Doomsday encounters standard, street-to-city-tier Avengers, the battle mirrors his slaughter of the Justice League International in 1992:

```text
[Casualty Assessment: Wave 1]
├── Captain America (Steve Rogers) ── Shield can absorb kinetic shock, but bone spikes crush ribs
├── Hawkeye & Black Widow ───────── Completely ineffective; human biology obliterated in seconds
├── Spider-Man (Peter Parker) ────── Evasion buys time, but a single glancing blow is catastrophic
└── Iron Man (Standard Armor) ───── Repulsors barely scratch; armor shredded like tin foil
```

* In *Superman* #75, Doomsday defeated Bloodwynd, Guy Gardner, Booster Gold, Blue Beetle, and Maxima with one arm literally tied behind his back.
* Standard physical weapons, conventional explosives, and standard Stark repulsors cannot penetrate Doomsday’s hardened calcium hide.

---

## 2. Wave 2: The Physical Heavyweights (Hulk & Thor)

When the Avengers deploy their cosmic powerhouses—**The Incredible Hulk** and **Thor (God of Thunder)**—the battle shifts into an apocalyptic slugfest capable of leveling continents.

### Thor vs. Doomsday
* **Thor’s Arsenal:** Divine strength, planetary-tier blunt striking with Mjolnir, weather manipulation, and divine lightning.
* **The Dynamic:** Thor’s lightning and concussive hammer blows will wound Doomsday initially. However, in *Hunter/Prey*, Doomsday survived Radiant’s concentrated energy blasts (which previously incinerated him) and developed immunity.
* If Thor treats Doomsday merely as a monster to bludgeon, Doomsday will adapt to Asgardian kinetic strikes and eventually wear Thor down through sheer, unyielding endurance.

### The Incredible Hulk vs. Doomsday
* **The Paradox:** Hulk gets stronger the angrier he gets; Doomsday adapts and mutates to counter the specific offensive vector hurting him.
* **World Breaker / Immortal Hulk:** At peak fury, Hulk can shatter tectonic plates with a footstep and regenerate entire limbs in milliseconds.
* Neither can kill the other through brute force: Doomsday’s reactive evolution negates blunt trauma, while Hulk’s connection to the Below-Place (*Immortal Hulk*) ensures Bruce Banner resurrects via the Green Door every time he dies.

---

## 3. Wave 3: The Magic & Reality Warpers (The Avengers Win)

The fundamental tactical error in combating Doomsday is attempting to defeat him through **physical trauma**. Physical trauma is literally the evolutionary catalyst that makes Doomsday stronger.

The Avengers win because their roster includes high-tier **mystics and reality-warpers** whose power bypasses biology entirely:

### A. Doctor Strange (Sorcerer Supreme)
* **Dimensional Banishment:** Strange does not need to hurt Doomsday. Using the **Mirror Dimension**, the Crimson Bands of Cyttorak, or the Dark Dimension gateways, Strange can exile Doomsday outside spacetime.
* **Astral Extraction:** In comic lore, Doomsday's consciousness is driven by pure instinct and agony. Strange can sever his astral form or place his consciousness in a permanent slumber.

### B. Wanda Maximoff (Scarlet Witch)
* **Chaos Magic:** Wanda’s probability manipulation alters fundamental reality. In *House of M*, she stripped millions of mutants of their genes with three whispered words.
* Wanda can alter Doomsday’s cellular biology: turning his bone spikes into chalk, reversing his evolutionary adaptations, or erasing his cellular resurrection factor entirely.

### C. The Sentry (Robert Reynolds)
* Possessing the "power of a million exploding suns" and god-tier **molecular manipulation**, Sentry can physically overpower Doomsday, fly him into the core of the Sun, or rip his molecules apart at the subatomic level—similar to how Sentry resurrected himself after Molecule Man dismantled him.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Captain America's shield block Doomsday's strikes?
Yes. Vibranium-adamantium alloy is virtually indestructible to physical blunt force and absorbs kinetic vibrational energy. However, while the shield itself will not break, the concussive shockwave from Doomsday's strikes would shatter Steve Rogers' human skeleton through kinetic transfer over sustained impact.

### Can Doomsday adapt to magic?
Doomsday has shown limited resistance to energy-based attacks after resurrection, but true reality warping and conceptual magic (like Scarlet Witch’s Chaos Magic or the Living Tribunal's judgment) operate outside the biological paradigm of Darwinian evolution. You cannot biologically adapt to your existence being rewritten from history.

### What if Doomsday fights the MCU Avengers?
Against the MCU Phase 1–3 Avengers (2012–2019), Doomsday would likely defeat the entire team unless Doctor Strange deployed the Time Stone (Eye of Agamotto) to trap Doomsday in an infinite time loop, identical to his defeat of Dormammu.
