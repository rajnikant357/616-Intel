## The Fatal Clash: Can Sentry Void Defeat Knull?

In *King in Black #1*, comic readers witnessed one of the most shocking moments in modern Marvel history: **Knull grabbed Sentry and ripped him clean in half.**

Why did this happen, and can Sentry Void defeat Knull under any circumstances?

### Why Knull Dominated Sentry:
1. **The Eldritch Symbiote Abyss:** Knull is not just an alien; he is the primordial god of the living abyss who existed before the Big Bang. Sentry’s Void entity is composed of darkness that Knull essentially *fathered*.
2. **Knull Consumed The Void:** When Sentry unleashed The Void, Knull did not fear it; he recognized it as part of his native element and absorbed it like water into an ocean.

### Could Sentry Void Win with Light Magic?
Only the **Enigma Force (Captain Universe)** wielded by Eddie Brock proved capable of slaying Knull. Because Sentry’s core nature is tied to the dark Void, Sentry was inherently vulnerable to the King in Black.
