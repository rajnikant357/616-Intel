## Power Analysis: Can The Void Beat Doctor Doom?

When fans ask **"Can The Void beat Doctor Doom?"**, the short answer is **yes, in a sudden encounter, but almost certainly no if Doom has preparation time.**

The Void is one of the most destructive forces in the Marvel cosmos, operating far above standard Avengers-tier heavyweights. Here is how the battle unfolds under different conditions:

### 1. The Spontaneous Encounter: The Void Wins (75% Probability)
In an unscripted clash where The Void ambushes Doctor Doom:
- The Void moves at superluminal speeds, instantly surrounding Doom with dark tendrils that disintegrate matter on contact.
- Doom's standard armor shields can absorb stellar radiation, but The Void attacks both the physical body and the astral consciousness simultaneously.
- Without custom wards prepared for Sentry’s unique quantum-molecular frequency, Doom’s physical form is torn apart.

### 2. The Prepared Encounter: Doctor Doom Wins (85% Probability)
When Doctor Doom knows The Void is coming:
- Doom exploits the psychological duality between Robert Reynolds and The Void using neuro-disrupters.
- Doom erects mystical exiles channeled through the Dark Dimension.
- Doom deploys cosmic siphons to absorb The Void’s dark energy directly into his power grid.
