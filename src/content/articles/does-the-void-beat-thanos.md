## Cosmic Showdown: Does The Void Beat Thanos?

A premier debate in Marvel powerscaling is **"Does The Void beat Thanos?"** The answer depends entirely on whether the Mad Titan possesses his cosmic artifacts.

### 1. The Void vs Base Thanos: The Void Wins (8/10)
Without the Infinity Gauntlet or Cosmic Cube:
- Thanos possesses immense Eternal durability and cosmic energy blasts, but Sentry/The Void operates on an unstable subatomic tier.
- When Sentry tore Ares in half and decomposed Owen Reece, he demonstrated that raw physical durability is meaningless against his molecular touch.
- The Void's shadow tendrils can pierce Thanos' personal forcefields and overwhelm him through attrition.

### 2. The Void vs Thanos with Infinity Gauntlet: Thanos Wins (10/10)
Wielding all six Infinity Stones:
- Thanos commands reality, time, space, soul, mind, and power.
- The Infinity Gauntlet operates above universal abstract entities. Thanos turns The Void into harmless mist with a thought.
