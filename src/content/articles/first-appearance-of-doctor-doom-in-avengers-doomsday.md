### Beat-by-Beat Scene Breakdown

1. **The Silence of Doomstadt**: The scene opens high in the snow-capped mountain peaks overlooking the fortified city of Doomstadt. Down in the valley, hundreds of Latverian citizens stand in silence outside the parapets of Castle Doom as the night sky pulses with unnatural violet and crimson incursion auroras.
2. **The Throne Room Antechamber**: Inside the colossal cathedral-like hall, banners displaying the Latverian royal crest hang between towering arched windows. Surrounding the perimeter stand silent, motionless **Doombots**—armored automaton guards clad in heavy steel plating, their mechanical optics glowing a faint, cold amber.
3. **The Slow Reveal of the Cowl**: The camera glides down the long granite floor toward the throne. Seated upright is a cloaked figure. The camera lingers on the textured folds of a rich, deep-emerald green hooded cloak, fastened across the chest by dual golden medallions linked by a heavy chain.
4. **The Forged Mask**: A robotic lieutenant approaches, projecting a holographic feed of collapsing multiversal timelines. The cloaked figure slowly raises his head. Beneath the shadow of the emerald cowl gleams the **forged titanium mask of Doctor Doom**—sculpted with cold, austere cheekbones, a riveted brow, and an immovable, unyielding mouth slit.
5. **The First Spoken Command**: In a low, resonant, aristocratic voice that carries zero traces of Tony Stark’s playful inflection, Doom speaks his first words:
   > *"Let the pretenders panic in their glass towers. When reality snaps beneath their weight, they will learn that order cannot be begged for. It must be commanded."*

With a subtle raise of his armored gauntlet, green runic sorcery ignites along the iron knuckles, and the holographic timelines are forcibly rearranged into a singular, controlled convergence point.
