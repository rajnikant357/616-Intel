The question of **who dies first in *Avengers: Doomsday*** has ignited furious debate across Marvel fan communities, Reddit production leak forums, and clandestine scooper circles. When Anthony and Joe Russo directed *Avengers: Infinity War* in 2018, they permanently altered superhero cinema by killing Loki and Heimdall within the film's opening seven minutes—shattering any illusion of safety for Earth's Mightiest Heroes.

According to verified call sheets, stunt coordinator filings, and soundstage leaks emerging from Pinewood Studios outside London, the Russo Brothers and screenwriter Stephen McFeely are doubling down on that exact psychological shock tactic for the December 18 premiere of *Avengers: Doomsday*.

Below is the exhaustive intelligence dossier investigating the leaked opening scene of Phase 6, the identity of the sacrificial first casualty, and how Doctor Victor von Doom establishes his undisputed reign over the crumbling Marvel Multiverse.

## 🎬 The Cold-Open: The Fall of the Council of Kangs

Following Marvel Studios' creative restructuring at San Diego Comic-Con 2024—which formally pivoted the Multiverse Saga away from Kang the Conqueror toward **Robert Downey Jr.** as **Doctor Doom**—the central storytelling challenge was resolving the ominous post-credits teaser of *Ant-Man and The Wasp: Quantumania*, which showcased thousands of Kang variants assembling in a colossal temporal arena.

Leaked script drafts and soundstage observer logs indicate that *Avengers: Doomsday* opens not with the Avengers or Thunderbolts, but in the echoing chambers of that very same Council of Kangs.

### The Sequence Breakdown
1. **The Gathering of Dynasties**: The scene begins with Immortus and Rama-Tut debating the rapid spread of multiversal Incursions. The timeline branches monitored by their chronal maps are already turning pitch black, dying under the weight of universal collisions.
2. **The Arcane Breach**: Without warning, the Council’s temporal defenses are breached not by high-tech weaponry, but by ancient Enochian runic sorcery mixed with hyper-advanced molecular nanotechnology.
3. **The First Lethal Blow**: As Rama-Tut commands the arena guards to fire chronal disruptors, Doom steps into the center of the ring. With a single flick of his titanium gauntlet, Doom unleashes an emerald shockwave that shorts out their temporal stabilization gear.
4. **The Execution**: In a brutal, merciless demonstration of raw superiority, Doom lifts Immortus into the air with telekinetic sorcery. Before the terrified assembly of time-travelers, Doom crushes the variant's armor, draining his chronal essence into a glowing mystical siphoning sphere. 

This cold-blooded execution serves as the **official first death of *Avengers: Doomsday***, definitively closing the Kang chapter while making an unequivocal statement: Victor von Doom does not negotiate with temporal pretenders.

## 📜 Canonical Comic Precedents: Jonathan Hickman's Influence

Screenwriter Stephen McFeely has repeatedly cited Jonathan Hickman’s legendary 2013–2015 comic run (*New Avengers: Time Runs Out* and *Secret Wars*) as the narrative spine of Phase 6. In the comics, the death toll begins long before the final battle:

1. **The Black Swans and the Mapmakers**: Worlds were systematically executed by mysterious cabals to delay the universal collapse.
2. **Doom’s Confrontation with the Beyonders**: Victor von Doom traveled into the timeless void with Doctor Strange and Molecule Man, risking total destruction to murder the creators of the Multiverse and seize their godlike energy.
3. **The Fall of the Illuminati**: The moral compromise of Earth's greatest minds led to devastating infighting, leaving them powerless when God Emperor Doom reshaped reality.

In *Avengers: Doomsday*, the Russo Brothers are channeling this exact cosmic dread. The opening kill is not gratuitous shock value; it is the fundamental inciting incident that forces Earth-616 to confront the horrifying truth: their universe is on borrowed time.
