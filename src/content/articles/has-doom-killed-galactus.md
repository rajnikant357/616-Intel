## Siphoning the Devourer: Has Doom Killed Galactus?

The audacious rivalry between mortal ruler Victor von Doom and cosmic entity Galactus has produced some of the greatest spectacles in Marvel Comics. 

### 1. Secret Wars (1984): Draining the World-Eater
In the original Jim Shooter *Secret Wars*, Doctor Doom infiltrated Galactus’ flagship, deciphered its celestial architecture, and constructed an energy-transfer harness. Doom siphoned Galactus' Power Cosmic into his own body, leaving Galactus incapacitated while Doom went on to confront the Beyonder.

### 2. Secret Wars (2015): Galactus as Doom’s Sentry
As God Emperor Doom, Victor's power was so absolute that he subdued Galactus entirely, re-purposing the Devourer of Worlds as an oversized gargoyle/sentinel standing guard over his citadel on Battleworld.

### Verdict:
While standard base Doctor Doom cannot overpower Galactus in an unassisted fistfight, Doom’s intellectual history proves he can systematically drain, defeat, and subjugate Galactus using cosmic siphon engineering.
