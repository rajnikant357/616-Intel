## The Survival Guide: How Will Dr Doom Survive The Void?

The Void is not merely a powerful supervillain; it is a malevolent primordial entity of pure existential entropy housed inside Robert Reynolds (Sentry). The Void has broken the limbs of the Hulk, ripped Ares in half, and laid waste to Asgard during *Siege*.

If Doctor Doom confronts The Void in comic canon or the MCU, **how will Dr Doom survive Void?**

---

## 1. The Strategy of Banishment Over Brawling

Victor von Doom is arrogant, but he is never foolish. He knows that attempting to trade physical blows with The Void is a death sentence. To survive and prevail, Doom relies on three strategic pillars:

1. **Dimensional Phase Shifting:** Doom’s armor incorporates micro-jump drives that phase his physical atoms out of synch with standard 3D spacetime. Even if The Void's shadow tendrils smash through Castle Doom, they phase through Doom's hard-light holographic avatar.
2. **The Crimson Bands of Cyttorak:** By invoking Cyttorak, Doom channels mystical bonds that bind entities of physical and astral malice, arresting The Void's forward momentum long enough to initiate ritual exiles.
3. **The Mirror Dimension & Dark Dimension Containment:** Rather than attempting to destroy an indestructible primordial entity, Doom routes The Void through gateway portals directly into barren dimensional corridors.

---

## 2. Exploiting Robert Reynolds’ Fragile Mind

The Void’s fatal vulnerability is its symbiotic link to the fractured consciousness of Robert Reynolds. 
- Doom is a master of neuro-telepathic circuitry. His armor can broadcast agoraphobic neuro-frequencies tuned specifically to Bob Reynolds' brainwaves.
- By triggering Reynolds' subconscious guilt and fear of his own monstrosity, Doom forces the persona of Sentry to reassert itself, suffocating The Void from within.
