## The Blockbuster Benchmark: Is Avengers: Doomsday Bigger Than Endgame?

In April 2019, *Avengers: Endgame* rewrote cinematic history. Grossing **$2.798 billion** worldwide, it shattered box office records, delivered an unprecedented emotional payoff to the 11-year Infinity Saga, and united over 40 superhero characters on screen in the Battle of Earth.

Seven years later, Marvel Studios is preparing its monumental follow-up: **Avengers: Doomsday (May 2026)**.

With the historic return of **Anthony and Joe Russo** to the director’s chair and **Robert Downey Jr.** returning to lead the franchise not as Iron Man, but as **Victor von Doom**, fans and industry analysts are asking one critical question: **Is Avengers: Doomsday bigger than Endgame?**

---

## 1. Budget & Talent Compensation: A Historic Financial Scale

When comparing financial commitments, *Avengers: Doomsday* is officially the most expensive cinematic endeavor in Disney history:

| Financial Metric | Avengers: Endgame (2019) | Avengers: Doomsday (2026 Projected) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Production Budget** | ~$356 Million (Net) | Projected **$400M–$450M** |
| **Director Compensation** | ~$25M + Backend Points (Russo Bros) | **$80 Million** Flat + Escalators (Russo Bros) |
| **Lead Star Compensation** | ~$75 Million Total (RDJ as Iron Man) | **$100+ Million** Guaranteed + Private Jet / Trailer Perks (RDJ as Doom) |
| **Filming Format & Soundstages** | Trilith Studios (Atlanta) | Pinewood Studios (London) + Atlanta + Virtual StageCraft |

According to reports from *Variety* and *The Hollywood Reporter*, Marvel Studios parent company Disney agreed to unprecedented compensation terms to secure RDJ and the Russos after the restructuring of Phase 6. In pure dollar-for-dollar investment, **Avengers: Doomsday surpasses Endgame before a single frame is even shot.**

## 2. Cast Size & Multiverse Complexity: Beyond Earth-616

In *Avengers: Endgame*, the ensemble consisted primarily of characters established within the singular timeline of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (Earth-616):

In *Avengers: Doomsday*, the narrative stakes of **Incursions** and the collapse of the Multiverse expand the casting pool across three separate cinematic eras:


While *Endgame* focused on mourning the fallen and pulling off a temporal heist, *Doomsday* is an interdimensional collision where multiple realities clash for survival, setting up the ultimate destruction of the timeline in *Avengers: Secret Wars*.

## 3. Narrative Stakes: The Decimation vs. Multiversal Extinction

To determine which movie has "bigger" stakes, we must evaluate the narrative consequences:

* **Endgame’s Stakes:** Thanos eliminated 50% of all life in one universe. The Avengers fought to reverse that loss and restore universal balance.
* **Doomsday’s Stakes:** The entire Multiverse is experiencing **Incursions**—phenomena where two universes collide at their focal points, obliterating both realities simultaneously.
* If the Avengers fail in *Doomsday*, it will not merely wipe out half of life—it will result in **the total annihilation of all alternate timelines and dimensions**, forcing Victor von Doom to salvage the fragments into Battleworld.

On a purely cosmic powerscaling level, the narrative crisis in *Doomsday* is infinitely more expansive than the Infinity Saga.

## 4. The Box Office Challenge: Can Doomsday Surpass $2.8 Billion?

While *Avengers: Doomsday* is bigger in budget, cast scope, and multiversal stakes, **surpassing Endgame's $2.798 billion worldwide box office remains a monumental hurdle**:

### The Tailwinds for Doomsday:
1. **The RDJ Factor:** Robert Downey Jr.'s return is the single most viral pop culture casting reveal since Harrison Ford returned for *Star Wars: The Force Awakens*.
2. **The Russo Brothers Track Record:** The Russos have directed four consecutive MCU masterworks (*Winter Soldier*, *Civil War*, *Infinity War*, *Endgame*), averaging over $1.7 billion per Avengers installment.
3. **Six-Year Avengers Drought:** By May 2026, seven years will have passed since *Endgame*. The pent-up global demand for a true Avengers team-up movie will be unprecedented.

### The Headwinds in the 2026 Market:
1. **Theatrical Landscape:** Post-2020 theatergoing habits, the decline of foreign box office in certain territories (notably China), and shorter streaming windows make crossing $2.5 billion vastly more difficult today than in 2019.
2. **Franchise Fatigue:** Unlike in 2019 when the MCU enjoyed nearly universal acclaim, Phase 4 and 5 experienced mixed audience reception, meaning *Doomsday* must win back casual moviegoers.

**Box Office Forecast:** Realistic industry consensus projects *Avengers: Doomsday* to open between $220M–$260M domestic and finish with a worldwide gross between **$1.8 Billion and $2.3 Billion**—putting it in elite company with *Infinity War*, even if matching *Endgame*'s historic $2.8 billion remains challenging.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Avengers: Doomsday filming back-to-back with Secret Wars?
Yes. Marvel Studios and the Russo Brothers are scheduling production across late 2025 and 2026, filming *Avengers: Doomsday* (scheduled for May 1, 2026) and *Avengers: Secret Wars* (scheduled for May 7, 2027) with continuous principal photography in the United Kingdom.

### Why was Kang the Conqueror replaced by Doctor Doom?
Following legal developments concerning actor Jonathan Majors in late 2023, Marvel Studios officially parted ways with the actor and pivoted its creative direction away from *Avengers: The Kang Dynasty*, re-centering Phase 6 around Victor von Doom and the iconic *Secret Wars* comic lore.

### Will the original Avengers return in Doomsday?
While Steve Rogers (Chris Evans) and Natasha Romanoff (Scarlett Johansson) concluded their arcs in *Endgame*, Chris Hemsworth (Thor) and Mark Ruffalo (Hulk) remain active. Multiversal cameos and variant appearances from original cast members remain a persistent rumor for *Doomsday* and *Secret Wars*.
