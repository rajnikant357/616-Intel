## Escaping the Grave: Is Doctor Doom Immortal?

Biologically, Victor von Doom was born a mortal human in the Latverian wilderness. Yet across decades of comic history, Doom has survived lethal execution, physical atomization, and chronological exile.

**Is Doctor Doom immortal?**

### The Four Methods of Doom's Functional Immortality:
1. **The Ovoid Mind Transfer:** Taught to Doom by the alien Ovoid race, this psychic discipline allows Doom to transfer his entire consciousness and soul into another living being simply by making direct eye contact at the moment of bodily destruction.
2. **Mystical Longevity Elixirs:** As a master sorcerer, Doom has prolonged his biological lifespan by centuries through Tibetan alchemy and blood-pact wards.
3. **The Time Platform:** When facing unavoidable disaster, Doom can jump forward or backward through history, rendering chronological aging irrelevant.
4. **Autonomous Doombot Redundancy:** Hundreds of Doombots—each believing it is the real Victor von Doom—ensure that Doom’s imperial continuity never terminates.
