## Cosmic Devourer vs Mortal Monarch: Is Galactus Afraid of Dr. Doom?

Galactus is a fundamental force of universal balance who feeds on entire biospheres. To ask whether Galactus is afraid of Doctor Doom requires distinguishing between **fear** and **deep tactical wariness**.

### What Galactus Thinks of Doom:
1. **In Raw Power:** Galactus does not fear Doom. At base levels, Galactus could incinerate Latveria from orbit with a gesture.
2. **In Technological Ingenuity:** Galactus **respects and monitors** Doom. In the original 1984 *Secret Wars*, Doom used the technology of Galactus’ worldship against him, temporarily stealing his cosmic essence.
3. **During 2015 Secret Wars:** God Emperor Doom turned Galactus into a giant mechanical security perimeter around Castle Doom on Battleworld.
