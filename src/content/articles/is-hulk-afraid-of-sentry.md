## Emotional Bonds and Cosmic Rage: Is Hulk Afraid of Sentry?

The relationship between **the Incredible Hulk** and **the Sentry** is one of the most poignant dynamics in Marvel Comics. Hulk is notorious for fearing almost nothing—so **is Hulk afraid of Sentry?**

### 1. Sentry as the "Golden Guardian": A Pacifying Light
In early Sentry appearances, Robert Reynolds' golden aura possessed a unique bio-resonant frequency that could calm the Savage Hulk into reverting to Bruce Banner. Hulk referred to Sentry as his only real friend among Earth's heroes: *"Golden Man nice to Hulk."*

### 2. World War Hulk: The Mutual Burnout
In *World War Hulk #5*, when Hulk returned to Earth seeking vengeance against the Illuminati, only Sentry could stand against him. Their brawl released energy capable of cracking Manhattan in half. Both pushed each other so hard that Sentry’s golden form burned out and Hulk reverted to an exhausted Bruce Banner, ending in mutual respect.

### 3. Fear of The Void
While Hulk does not fear Sentry, **Hulk is deeply terrified of The Void**. In *The Sentry (Vol. 2)*, The Void snapped Hulk’s limbs like twigs and forced Hulk to confront pure psychic despair.
