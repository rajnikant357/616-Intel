## 1. Is Iron Man the Doctor Doom?

The short answer is **no, but with a monumental multiversal twist**.

When Kevin Feige introduced Robert Downey Jr. in July 2024, he explicitly introduced him with a single name: **Victor von Doom**. Marvel Studios has gone out of its way in official press releases to state that Earth-616’s Tony Stark remains dead. His legendary sacrifice in *Avengers: Endgame*—wielding the Infinity Stones to defeat Thanos and save the universe—will **not** be retconned or cheapened through simple magical resurrection.

```
                  THE IDENTITY DICHOTOMY
                  
     [ Earth-616 Tony Stark ]            [ MCU Victor von Doom ]
                |                                    |
     Born in Manhattan, New York         Born in Latveria, Eastern Europe
     Billionaire Industrialist           Aristocratic Sorcerer & Monarch
     Sacrificed Life in Endgame          Reconstructs Multiverse into Battleworld
     Protective Heroic Heart             Ruthless Tyrannical Will
```

However, having the exact same actor play both characters cannot be brushed aside as coincidental casting. In a visual medium where audiences have spent 15 years emotionally bonding with Robert Downey Jr.’s face, the psychological horror of seeing that face beneath Doom's mask is the entire point. 

When heroes like **Tom Holland’s Spider-Man**, **James Rhodes (War Machine)**, or **Pepper Potts** inevitably gaze upon Victor von Doom, they will not see an unfamiliar Latverian monarch—they will see the ghost of their mentor, brother, and savior staring back with cold, merciless, emerald-lit eyes.

## 2. Will Loki Going to Die in *Avengers: Doomsday*?

According to circulating script breakdowns and closed-door story room leaks, **Loki is marked for a tragic, event-level departure in *Avengers: Doomsday***.

To understand why Loki must fall, look at his current canonical position at the climax of *Loki* Season 2:
- Loki destroyed the Temporal Loom with his bare hands.
- He took his place upon the throne at the Citadel at the End of Time.
- He used his green temporal magic to weave the infinite dying timeline branches into a living, flourishing **Multiversal World Tree (Yggdrasil)**.
- He chose eternal loneliness, physically holding all reality together so that his friends and infinite souls could have a chance at free will.

```
               THE TEMPORAL SIEGE OF YGGDRASIL
               
            [ Loki: God of Stories on the Throne ]
                              |
                 Holds Infinite Timelines
                              |
               <<< DOCTOR DOOM'S ASSAULT >>>
                              |
    - Siphons Chronal Energy from Temporal Threads
    - Severs Root Branches with Molecular Disrupters
    - Forces Loki to Release His Hold or Burn
                              |
      [ Catastrophic Singularity: BATTLEWORLD FORMED ]
```

### Why Doom Must Destroy Loki’s Throne
In Jonathan Hickman’s *Secret Wars*, Doctor Doom cannot construct **Battleworld** as long as the multiverse functions naturally. The multiverse must collapse completely to leave only dying planetary fragments behind.

Because Loki is the physical anchor keeping the multiverse alive, **he is the primary obstacle to Doctor Doom's ascension**. 

Leaked story treatments describe an earth-shattering sequence where Doctor Doom breaches the End of Time. Recognizing that Loki is holding the cosmic threads together, Doom confronts the God of Stories. Rather than a quick defeat, leaks suggest Loki makes a heroic last stand, channeling every ounce of his godhood to hold the branches open just long enough for a small faction of heroes (including Spider-Man, Reed Richards, and Wolverine) to escape into a dimensional life raft before his physical form dissolves into the temporal winds.

### How the Fight Unfolds
1. **The Incursion of the Citadel**: Doom utilizes a device derived from salvaged Kang technology and arcane runes to locate the timeless dimension where Loki reigns.

3. **The Technological Counter**: When Loki attempts to time-slip Doom or bind him in temporal loops, Doom activates his armor's chronal dampers, neutralizing the temporal distortion.
4. **The Siphoning**: Doom embeds molecular siphons into the roots of Yggdrasil. The glowing green branches are stripped of their life energy, turning black as Doom absorbs the infinite cosmic power required to forge Battleworld.

## 4. Is Doctor Doom a Variant of Tony Stark (Iron Man)?

The exact relationship between **Victor von Doom** and **Tony Stark** is the most closely guarded secret in Marvel Studios history. While Kevin Feige presented him as "Victor von Doom," high-credibility scoops from *The Cosmic Circus*, *MyTimeToShineHello*, and *Alex Perez* confirm that the narrative acknowledges their shared physical likeness.

There are three primary narrative models being explored across script revisions:

### Model A: The Multiverse Karmic Variant (The Divergent Bloodline)
In an alternate timeline—rumored to be the retro-future Earth where *The Fantastic Four: First Steps* originates or a nearby divergent reality—the child who would have become Tony Stark was born into or adopted by the Latverian royal family following a multiversal displacement. Raised without Howard Stark's capitalism, this variant mastered both cybernetic engineering and the dark sorcery of his Latverian ancestors, taking the name **Victor von Doom**.

### Model B: The Comic-Accurate *Infamous Iron Man* Parallel
In Marvel Comics (2016's *Infamous Iron Man* by Brian Michael Bendis and Alex Maleev), Victor von Doom undergoes a moral crisis following *Secret Wars*. Believing that Tony Stark’s legacy was noble, Doom adopts the Iron Man moniker, builds his own high-tech suit fused with green sorcery, and attempts to become a hero through tyrannical means. 

Marvel Studios could invert this concept: an Iron Man from a collapsed reality who became disillusioned by loss, embraced dark sorcery, and crowned himself the Doctor of Doom.

```
       COMIC CANON PRECEDENT: EARTH-11020 & EXILES #23
       
   In comic continuity (Exiles #23), an alternate reality 
   showcased Tony Stark and Victor von Doom as college roommates 
   whose minds and destinies were swapped in a laboratory accident, 
   creating the armored dictator known as "Iron Man: Lord of Latveria."
```

### Model C: Pure Coincidence & Psychological Warfare
Victor von Doom is 100% biological Latverian royalty, but multiversal infinite probability dictates that in certain realities, genetic configurations duplicate. Doom is fully aware of Earth-616’s fallen hero and weaponizes that exact visual resemblance to psychologically paralyze the Avengers during combat, mocking their reverence for a dead mortal.
