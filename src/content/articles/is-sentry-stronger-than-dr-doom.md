## Strength Comparison: Is Sentry Stronger Than Dr. Doom?

When evaluating **"Is Sentry stronger than Dr. Doom?"**, the distinction between **pure physical strength** and **overall combat versatility** is paramount.

### Pure Physical Strength: Sentry Outclasses Doom (10/10)
- **Sentry's Physical Might:** Sentry operates with unquantifiable planetary physical strength. He stopped a strike from the World-Breaker Hulk, tore God of War Ares in half, and shattered Terrax's cosmic axe with two fingers.
- **Doctor Doom's Physical Might:** While Doom’s armor amplifies his mortal strength to lift around 2 to 5 tons, he cannot physically trade punches with high-tier bruisers like Sentry, Thor, or the Hulk without augmenting himself with mystical enchantments.

### Overall Lethality
While Sentry possesses vastly superior physical muscles and subatomic regeneration, Doctor Doom's intellect, magical wards, and prep-time technology make Doom far more dangerous in prolonged multiversal warfare.
