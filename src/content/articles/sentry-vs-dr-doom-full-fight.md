## The Canonical Showdown: Sentry (Santry) vs Doctor Doom

The question of **Santry vs Dr Doom** (Sentry vs Doctor Doom) represents one of the most explosive debates in Marvel Comics history. On one side stands Robert Reynolds, the Golden Guardian endowed with the power of *one million exploding suns*. On the other stands Victor von Doom, monarch of Latveria, master of dark sorcery, and scientific polymath second to none.

In comic book continuity, this fight actually happened with devastating consequences across Castle Doom.

## Mighty Avengers #9–11: The Invasion of Latveria

Following a biotoxin Symbiote bomb detonation over Manhattan attributed to Latverian technology, Tony Stark leads the Mighty Avengers into Latveria. Leading the vanguard is none other than Sentry.

### Round-by-Round Fight Breakdown:

1. **Breaching Castle Doom's Defensive Array:**
   Doctor Doom activates Latveria’s orbital defense satellites and anti-matter particle shields. While Iron Man and Ms. Marvel struggle to penetrate the kinetic perimeter, Sentry flies through the orbital defenses like paper, shattering Castle Doom's primary mystical barriers with pure physical velocity.

2. **The Direct Physical Clash:**
   Doctor Doom confronts Sentry inside his inner sanctum, unleashing high-voltage mystical blasts fueled by the flames of the Faltine. Sentry absorbs the blast with negligible injury, his golden aura flaring.

3. **Ripping the Iron Mask:**
   In an iconic panel illustrated by Mark Bagley, Sentry grips Doctor Doom’s armored helmet with bare hands. Exerting Class 100+ torque, Sentry shears through the titanium-infused alloy, ripping the faceplate clean off Doom's face and exposing the dictator to his own imperial guard.

4. **Doom's Tactical Countermeasure:**
   Recognizing that physical brawling against Sentry is suicidal, Doom deploys chronal displacers and contingency Doombots, teleporting the core Avengers across time while retreating into subterranean bunkers.
