## The Ultimate Ensemble: Star Cast of Avengers: Doomsday (2026)

When Marvel Studios took the stage at San Diego Comic-Con’s Hall H in July 2024, Kevin Feige delivered the most shocking and consequential casting announcement in modern cinematic history: **Robert Downey Jr. is returning to the MCU to star as Doctor Victor von Doom** in **Avengers: Doomsday**, set to hit theaters on **May 1, 2026**.

Helmed by visionary directors **Anthony and Joe Russo** and scripted by *Endgame* co-writer **Stephen McFeely**, *Avengers: Doomsday* unites over sixty characters across multiple superhero factions and parallel realities.

Here is the comprehensive, verified directory of the **star cast of Avengers: Doomsday**, detailing confirmed leads, returning legacy Avengers, the Fantastic Four, Thunderbolts recruits, and high-profile multiversal cameos.

---

## 1. The Headliner: Doctor Victor von Doom

### Robert Downey Jr. as Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom
* **The Role:** The central anchor and supreme antagonist of Phase 6. Victor von Doom is a monarch, sorcerer, and scientific genius from an alternate universe who seeks to save—or reshape—the dying Multiverse on his own totalitarian terms.
* **Production Context:** Following a five-year hiatus from Marvel following *Avengers: Endgame* and his Academy Award win for *Oppenheimer*, Downey secured a landmark deal exceeding $100 million plus performance incentives to headline *Doomsday* and *Secret Wars*.

## 3. Returning Core Avengers & Guardians

Several foundational MCU stars are locked in and reported to return as Earth’s primary defenders against the Incursion crisis:

### Benedict Cumberbatch as Doctor Stephen Strange
* Following the events of *Multiverse of Madness*, Strange is currently navigating Incursions in the Dark Dimension alongside Clea (Charlize Theron). He serves as the primary sorcery and multiversal strategist for the Avengers.

### Chris Hemsworth as Thor Odinson
* The Asgardian God of Thunder returns following *Thor: Love and Thunder*. Thor’s raw divine fury and mastery of lightning with Stormbreaker position him as one of the few physical heavyweights capable of damaging Doom’s vanguard.

### Anthony Mackie as Sam Wilson / Captain America
* Fresh off the theatrical release of *Captain America: Brave New World*, Sam Wilson steps up to formally lead the newly reconstituted Avengers squad on the ground.

### Tom Holland as Peter Parker / Spider-Man
* Filming back-to-back alongside *Spider-Man 4*, Tom Holland’s Peter Parker will experience a profound psychological shock upon encountering Victor von Doom—a man possessing the identical face of his late mentor, Tony Stark.

### Sebastian Stan as Bucky Barnes / The Winter Soldier
* Transitioning from the leadership ranks of the Thunderbolts and Congressional politics back into frontline military combat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Robert Downey Jr. playing an evil Tony Stark variant or Victor von Doom?
At San Diego Comic-Con, director Joe Russo explicitly stated: *"If we are going to bring Victor von Doom to the screen... he is one of the most complex characters in all of comics. As proof of the unimaginable possibilities of the Marvel Multiverse, we give you the one person who could play Victor von Doom..."* Robert Downey Jr. is portraying **Victor von Doom**, though his physical resemblance to Tony Stark is expected to be a major psychological factor for the surviving Avengers.

### When will Avengers: Doomsday be released in theaters?
*Avengers: Doomsday* is officially scheduled for worldwide theatrical release on **May 1, 2026**. It will be followed one year later by *Avengers: Secret Wars* on **May 7, 2027**.

### Will Kang the Conqueror appear in Avengers: Doomsday?
No. Marvel Studios officially shifted away from the Kang storyline, renaming the fifth Avengers movie from *Avengers: The Kang Dynasty* to *Avengers: Doomsday* to focus entirely on Doctor Doom.
