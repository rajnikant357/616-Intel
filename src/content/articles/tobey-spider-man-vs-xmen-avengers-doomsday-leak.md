## Why Are They Fighting? The Moral Tragedy of Incursions

To understand why heroic figures like Tobey Maguire’s Spider-Man and Hugh Jackman’s Wolverine would come to blows, one must examine the canonical mechanics of Incursions established by writer Jonathan Hickman in Marvel Comics' 2013–2015 run of *New Avengers*:

> **The Universal Incursion Law:**  
> When the multiverse decays, two universes collide at a focal point: their respective Earths. For roughly eight hours, the boundary between them thins. If neither Earth is destroyed, **both universes are permanently erased**. If one Earth is destroyed before the collision completes, the surviving universe lives on.

This rule eliminates traditional hero-versus-villain dynamics and replaces them with an agonizing moral trolley problem. 

1. **Self-Defense and Misunderstanding:** From Peter Parker’s perspective, unknown multiversal intruders have entered his city while the sky literally falls. He is operating on pure protective instinct.
2. **Desperate Survival:** From the perspective of the Fox mutants, Earth-96283 represents an existential threat to Earth-10005. While Wolverine and the X-Men are heroes, the prospect of watching their entire reality turn to ash drives them into ruthless, desperate action.
3. **The Absence of a Third Way:** In standard superhero crossovers, heroes fight due to a misunderstanding, realize they are on the same side, and team up to defeat the real villain. Leaks emphasize that in *Avengers: Doomsday*, **there is no easy compromise**. The laws of physics dictate that one world must fall.

## Tactical Breakdown: Tobey's Spider-Man vs Wolverine & Fox Mutants

The leaked fight choreography described by insiders promises a brutal, kinetic, and emotionally draining masterclass in **[superhero action](/articles/superhero-action)** that contrasts Maguire's acrobatic agility with the raw, visceral violence of the Fox X-Men universe.

### 1. Maguire's Spider-Sense vs. Logan's Adamantium
* **Acrobatic Defense:** Peter Parker uses his organic webbing and unmatched environmental awareness to sling through the fracturing geometry of Manhattan, evading Wolverine's lunges and adamantium slashes.
* **Non-Lethal Restraint:** In true Peter Parker fashion, Tobey’s Spider-Man attempts to incapacitate rather than kill. Leaks state he traps Logan in multi-strand web nets and pins his arms using tension cables anchored between high-rises.
* **Wolverine's Relentless Advance:** Logan cuts through high-tensile webbing with ease, healing through kinetic strikes and forcing Spider-Man to push his Class-25 physical strength to its absolute limits just to parry bone-shattering claws.

### 2. The Chaos of Deadpool & Collateral Warfare
* Reports state Ryan Reynolds' Deadpool brings a grim, dark humor to the sequence, acknowledging the meta-tragedy of Sony and Fox timelines smashing together while simultaneously firing automatic weapons and attempting to hack through Spider-Man's defense.
* Additional mutant abilities (such as secondary energy blasts or telekinetic shockwaves from background X-Men) shred the structural integrity of the surrounding city, turning the battlefield into an inferno of collapsing glass, steel, and concrete.

### 3. Spider-Man’s Decisive Combat Experience
Having already battled Green Goblin, Doc Ock, Sandman, Venom, and multiversal villains in *Spider-Man: No Way Home*, Tobey’s Peter is no amateur. The leaks emphasize that he outmaneuvers the mutants tactically, using their momentum and the fluctuating gravity of the Incursion against them. Yet, as brilliantly as he fights, physical victory cannot solve a cosmic convergence.

## Climax of the Incursion: Annihilation & Doctor Doom’s Harvest

The fight does not end with a knockout punch. Instead, cosmic reality catches up with both teams.

### The Planetary Incursion Point
As the countdown to universal collision expires, the blue-white perimeter of the Incursion point collapses. Shockwaves rip through the Earth-96283 crust, tearing apart tectonic plates and disintegrating buildings into pure energy filaments.

Peter Parker desperately attempts to swing back toward Queens to reach Mary Jane and Mayday, but the atmosphere itself dissolves into a blinding vortex of cosmic static. The tragedy mirrors the iconic opening pages of 2015's *Secret Wars* #1, where the Ultimate Universe (Earth-1610) and Mainstream Universe (Earth-616) smash together in total mutual destruction.

### The Hand of Doctor Doom
At the final microsecond before total void, emerald sorcery and golden cosmic signatures interlock across the dying worlds. As established in leaks surrounding **[Robert Downey Jr.'s Victor von Doom](/articles/dr-doom-doomsday)**, Doctor Doom does not prevent the destruction of universes—he **harvests their remnants**. 

Using a synthesis of dark sorcery, temporal technology, and siphoned cosmic power, Doom plucks critical fragments, biomes, and select champions from doomed realities to stitch together his patchwork reality: **Battleworld**.
