## Beyond Latverian Arrogance: Who Can Defeat Dr. Doom Easily?

Doctor Victor von Doom is famous for toppling gods, stealing cosmic power, and defying celestial hierarchies. However, there are beings in the Marvel Universe whose sheer power scale renders Doom’s intellect and armor utterly trivial in a direct confrontation.

Here are the entities who can defeat Doctor Doom **easily**:

---

## 1. The One Above All
The supreme omnipotent consciousness of the Marvel Omniverse. Against The One Above All, no spell, Doombot, or siphon functions. Doom is simply an ink drawing subject to the author’s pen.

## 2. The Living Tribunal
The three-faced arbiter of cosmic law answers only to The One Above All. The Living Tribunal can nullify the Infinity Stones with a whisper and erase entire timelines. Standard Doctor Doom cannot harm the Tribunal.

## 3. Pre-Retcon Beyonder
During the original 1984 *Secret Wars*, the Beyonder was literally billions of times more powerful than the entire multiverse combined. While Doom eventually tricked him with technological syphons, in a direct spontaneous test of power, the Beyonder can blink Doom out of existence.

## 4. Owen Reece (Molecule Man)
Molecule Man is the living bomb created by the Beyonders to destroy universes. As demonstrated during Hickman’s *Secret Wars*, God Emperor Doom’s entire divine status was merely a loan of Owen Reece’s reality-warping juice.

## 5. Adult Franklin Richards
A fully realized Franklin Richards creates pocket universes beneath his bedsheets and turned Galactus into his personal herald. Franklin can rewrite Doom’s molecular structure into starlight in seconds.
