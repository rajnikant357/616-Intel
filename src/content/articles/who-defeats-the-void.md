## Toppling the Cosmic Nightmare: Who Defeats The Void?

The Void is widely regarded as one of the most indestructible forces in the Marvel Universe. Yet across pivotal comic events, a select few have managed to slay, banish, or devour Sentry's dark reflection.

---

## 1. Thor (God of Thunder) — *Siege #4*
At the climax of *Siege*, The Void tore Ares in half and reduced Asgard to ruins. Iron Man crashed the entire Helicarrier into The Void as a kinetic bullet, forcing Robert Reynolds to briefly regain consciousness. 
Weeping, Bob begged Thor to end his suffering before The Void reclaimed him. Thor summoned the full wrath of the heavens, striking Bob with a celestial bolt of lightning that reduced his skeleton to ash, before flying the remains into the heart of the Sun.

## 2. Knull (The King in Black) — *King in Black #1*
In one of the most ruthless power-scaling displays in comic history, Knull intercepted Sentry during an aerial charge, restrained him with symbiote darkness, and effortlessly ripped him in half, consuming The Void’s shadow into his collective consciousness.

## 3. Robert Reynolds Himself
The Void's greatest enemy has always been the heroic consciousness of Bob Reynolds. In Paul Jenkins' original *Sentry* (2000) miniseries, Sentry collaborated with Doctor Strange and Mister Fantastic to erase all memory of Sentry from the collective consciousness of mankind, effectively neutralizing The Void's existence for decades.
