## The Fate of the Ultimate Behemoth: Who Finally Kills Doomsday?

The name **Doomsday** strikes fear into the hearts of comic book fans. Whether discussing the genetically-engineered Kryptonian monster that killed the Man of Steel or the multiversal incursion crisis in Marvel’s *Avengers: Doomsday*, audiences continually ask: **Who finally kills Doomsday?**

---

### 1. Superman (1992: The Death of Superman)
In *Superman (Vol. 2) #75*, Superman and Doomsday engaged in an earth-shattering brawl across Ohio into the streets of Metropolis. In the final simultaneous exchange, Superman landed a desperate haymaker that snapped Doomsday’s neck, collapsing dead seconds later.

### 2. Imperiex (Our Worlds at War)
During the *Our Worlds at War* cosmic crossover, the cosmic conqueror Imperiex vaporized Doomsday with a single blast of Big Bang energy, leaving only a glowing skeleton.

### 3. Entropy at the End of Time (*Hunter/Prey*)
Realizing Doomsday evolves immunity to whatever previously killed him, Superman and Waverider transported Doomsday to the literal **End of Time**, where universal entropy shredded his molecular structure across eternity.
