## The Ultimate Nemesis: Who Is Doctor Doom's Greatest Enemy?

While Doctor Doom has clashed with the Avengers, the X-Men, Spider-Man, and cosmic entities, his singular, definitive archenemy will forever be **Reed Richards (Mister Fantastic)**.

---

### The Genesis of the Grudge
Their rivalry was born not on a battlefield, but in a dormitory at Empire State University:
- A young Victor von Doom engineered an interdimensional communication machine to reach his mother’s soul in Hell.
- Reed Richards reviewed Doom’s calculations and politely pointed out a flaw in the trans-spatial coordinates.
- Blinding pride drove Doom to ignore Reed's warning. The machine exploded, scarring Victor's face and cementing his lifelong delusion that Richards sabotaged him.

---

### The Climax of Secret Wars (2015)
The supreme culmination of their rivalry occurred on Battleworld. When Doom reconstructed reality, he took everything Reed loved: Sue Storm, the children, and the role of savior. Yet when Reed finally confronted him, Doom admitted:
> *"He would have done better than I."*

With those words, Molecule Man transferred the Beyonders' power to Reed Richards, who rebuilt the Marvel Multiverse and healed Victor’s scarred face, cementing their rivalry as the greatest philosophical conflict in comic history.
