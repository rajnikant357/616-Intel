## The Psychology of Fear: Who Is Dr. Doom Afraid Of?

Doctor Victor von Doom is defined by monumental hubris. He has stared down Galactus, mocked Thanos, and claimed the power of the Beyonders. Yet deep within his psyche, there are specific horrors that Victor von Doom genuinely fears.

---

## 1. Mephisto and the Damnation of Cynthia von Doom
For much of his adult life, Doom’s greatest source of dread was the Hell-lord Mephisto. Doom’s mother, Cynthia von Doom, made a bargain with Mephisto that cost her soul. 
Every Midsummer’s Eve, Doom was forced by ancient magical rite to challenge Mephisto for her soul, suffering agonizing mental and physical torment every year he failed. The fear of his mother suffering eternity in Hell was Doom’s emotional Achilles' heel until *Triumph and Torment*.

## 2. The Marquis of Death (Clyde Wyncham)
In Mark Millar’s *Fantastic Four* run (#566–569), Doom encountered his former master, the Marquis of Death. The Marquis possessed reality manipulation on such a horrific scale that he forced Doom to watch alternate Earths burn and cast Doom into the prehistoric ocean to be devoured by Megalodons.

## 3. Admitting Reed Richards Is Smarter
Psychologically, Victor von Doom’s deepest, most paralyzing terror is not physical destruction—it is acknowledging that Reed Richards (Mister Fantastic) is his intellectual and moral superior. At the conclusion of 2015’s *Secret Wars*, Doom finally admits to Molecule Man: *"He would have done better than I."*
