## Hierarchical Breakdown: Who Is More Powerful Than the Void in Marvel?

While The Void is a planetary-to-universal nightmare, the Marvel cosmos is inhabited by entities of multiversal and omniversal scale that dwarf Robert Reynolds’ shadow.

### Ranked Entities Above The Void:
1. **The One Above All:** The undisputed omnipotent creator.
2. **The Living Tribunal:** Multiversal judge who can nullify universal laws with a word.
3. **The Beyonders (Ivory Kings):** Trans-dimensional beings who wiped out the Celestials and the entire Living Tribunal hierarchy.
4. **Owen Reece (Molecule Man):** Holds multiversal reality in his palm; can unmake The Void's molecules instantaneously.
5. **Knull (King in Black):** Symbiote primordial god who physically overpowered Sentry and consumed The Void in *King in Black #1*.
6. **God Emperor Doom:** Victor von Doom empowered by the Beyonders during 2015 *Secret Wars*.
