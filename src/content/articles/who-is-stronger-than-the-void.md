## Cosmic Hierarchy: Who Is Stronger Than The Void?

The Void is one of the most frightening Omega-plus threats in Marvel lore. But as terrifying as Sentry’s shadow is, several beings sit higher on the universal food chain.

---

## 1. Knull, God of the Symbiotes
In *King in Black #1*, Knull provided definitive canonical proof of superiority. When Sentry attempted to fly Knull into orbit, Knull reversed the hold and ripped Sentry clean in half, devouring The Void’s shadow into his own symbiote ocean.

## 2. Molecule Man (Owen Reece)
While Sentry once disintegrated Owen Reece in *Dark Avengers #12*, Molecule Man was suffering from severe psychiatric suppression. At full power, Owen Reece controls the omniversal building blocks of all dimensions.

## 3. The Beyonders (The Ivory Kings)
The race of extradimensional entities who killed the Living Tribunal, the Celestials, and the abstract entities. The Void’s planetary-to-galaxy-tier reach cannot touch the Beyonders.

## 4. God Emperor Doom
Channeling the collected power of the Beyonders in 2015’s *Secret Wars*, Doom wielded omnipotent multiversal dominion that far outclassed The Void.
