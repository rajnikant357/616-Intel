## The Apex of Phase 6: Who Is the Strongest Person in Avengers: Doomsday?

As Marvel Studios hurtles toward **Avengers: Doomsday (May 2026)**, the Marvel Cinematic Universe faces a monumental power shift. The days when planetary threats like Loki’s Chitauri invasion or Ultron’s Sokovia meteor dominated the landscape are long gone.

With **Incursions** threatening to erase entire realities and multiversal borders collapsing, the characters clashing on screen possess powers capable of warping time, restructuring timelines, and shattering celestial dimensions.

When the dust settles, **who is truly the strongest person in Avengers: Doomsday?** Here is the definitive power tier ranking of confirmed and reported characters for Phase 6’s biggest spectacle.

---

## Tier 1: The Multiversal Sovereigns (God-Tier Omnipotence)

At the highest tier of power reside the beings who govern or threaten the fabric of the Multiverse itself.

### 1. Loki — The God of Stories
* **The Living Axis of Reality:** At the conclusion of *Loki* Season 2, Loki abandoned all personal ambition to save his friends and replaced He Who Remains' temporal loom.
* **Current Power Level:** Seated at the Citadel at the End of Time, Loki literally holds millions of dying multiversal branches in his hands, channeling green temporal magic to sustain life across the cosmic tree **Yggdrasil**.
* **Combat Assessment:** While Loki is currently tethered to his throne, his magical authority over temporal lines makes him the supreme entity in the current MCU. Any Incursion triggered by Doctor Doom directly threatens Loki’s divine web.

### 2. Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom (Robert Downey Jr.)
* **The Multiversal Mastermind:** When Robert Downey Jr. steps onto the screen as Doctor Doom, he will not merely be a man in an iron suit.
* **Anticipated Abilities:** Combining cosmic science, Latverian robotics, and mastery over dark sorcery that rivals or exceeds the Sorcerer Supreme, Doom understands the physics of Incursions better than anyone in existence.
* **The Ceiling:** If *Avengers: Doomsday* follows the legendary 2015 *Secret Wars* comic trajectory, Doom will ascend to **God Emperor Doom**—stealing multiversal energies to forge Battleworld from the dying ashes of reality.

---

## Tier 2: The Cosmic & Reality-Warping Heavyweights

These characters possess destructive power that can wipe out planetary populations or rewrite the laws of physics in real time:

### 3. The Sentry / The Void (Lewis Pullman)
* **Debut in *Thunderbolts***:** Introduced as Bob, the Sentry is confirmed to possess **the power of a million exploding suns**.
* **Destructive Scope:** In comic lore, Sentry’s dark persona, **The Void**, can effortlessly tear gods in half, consume planets in living darkness, and manipulate matter at the molecular level. If unleashed in *Doomsday*, Sentry is the physical trump card of the mortal realm.

### 4. Wanda Maximoff / The Scarlet Witch (Multiverse Incursion Return)
* **Chaos Magic Incarnate:** In *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*, Wanda effortlessly massacred Earth-838’s Illuminati (including Captain Carter, Black Bolt, and Professor X).
* **Current Status:** While buried under Mount Wundagore, comic and insider reports indicate her multiversal resurgence will position her as a cosmic anchor whose Chaos Magic can unravel or mend dying timelines.

### 5. Doctor Strange (Benedict Cumberbatch)
* **Clea & Darkhold Augmentation:** At the end of *Multiverse of Madness*, Strange opened his Third Eye and jumped into the Dark Dimension alongside Clea to repair an Incursion.
* Armed with forbidden dark sorcery, the Cloak of Levitation, and interdimensional mastery, Strange is Earth’s primary tactical defender against multiversal ruin.

## The Decisive Factor: Brains vs. Raw Energy

In *Avengers: Infinity War*, Thanos demonstrated that possessing raw power is meaningless without a calculated strategic framework.

In *Avengers: Doomsday*, while **The Sentry** and **Thor** hold immense physical and kinetic output, **Doctor Doom** will likely rank as the most dangerous and effectively "strongest" individual on screen because of his **intellectual weaponization of the Multiverse**:
* Doom does not need to physically overpower Thor in a wrestling match.
* By manipulating Incursion wave-harmonics, Doom can drop an alternate dying universe directly on top of his opponents, weaponizing the very fabric of existence.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Thor beat Doctor Doom in Avengers: Doomsday?
In direct hand-to-hand combat, Thor’s godly physiology and lightning strikes can shatter Doom’s standard armor. However, Doctor Doom will utilize mystical counter-wards, Doombot decoys, and temporal traps to neutralize Thor before Odinson can land a lethal blow with Stormbreaker.

### Will Franklin Richards appear in Avengers: Doomsday?
Rumors surrounding *The Fantastic Four: First Steps* and *Avengers: Doomsday* suggest that Franklin Richards—the mutant son of Reed and Sue Storm who can create entire universes in his hands—may be introduced, which would immediately position him as the most powerful reality-warper in the franchise.

### Is Doctor Doom stronger than Thanos?
Without cosmic artifacts, base Thanos has greater natural physical strength and durability than base Doctor Doom. However, Doctor Doom's mastery of dark magic, time travel, and multiversal engineering gives him a much higher tactical ceiling than Thanos, as demonstrated when God Emperor Doom tore Thanos's spine out in *Secret Wars*.
