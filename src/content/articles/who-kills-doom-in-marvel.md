## The Mortalities of a Monarch: Who Kills Doom in Marvel?

Doctor Victor von Doom is notoriously difficult to kill. Between his legion of autonomous Doombots, chronal displacements, and consciousness transfers, most "deaths" of Doctor Doom turn out to be decoys. 

However, across major Marvel storylines and alternate realities, Doom has met shocking, violent ends.

---

## 1. Thanos with the Infinity Gauntlet (*Infinity Gauntlet #4*)
When Doom attempted an ill-advised ambush against the Mad Titan wielding the six Infinity Gems, Thanos effortlessly turned Doom's titanium armor into fragile glass and shattered him into a million crystalline shards.

## 2. Morgan le Fay (*Dark Avengers #4*)
The ancient Arthurian sorceress Morgan le Fay traveled through time to Castle Doom, decapitated Victor von Doom in his own throne room, and left his severed head as a warning. Doom survived only because the Sentry and Iron Man used time-displacement technology to reverse the causality.

## 3. Reed Richards and the Soul Extraction (*Fantastic Four #508*)
Trapped in Hell by Doom's mystical schemes, Reed Richards absorbed the cosmic energies of the Mobius chamber and physically dismantled Doom's astral tether, consigning Doom to the infernal pits.

## 4. Terrax the Untamed (*Fantastic Four #260*)
Cosmic herald Terrax, restored by Doom to do his bidding, rebelled and used cosmic geomancy to disintegrate Doom’s biological vessel, forcing Doom to perform his famous Ovoid mind transfer into the body of an innocent onlooker, Norman McArthur.
