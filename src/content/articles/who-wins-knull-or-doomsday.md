## The Clash of Primordial Terrors: Knull vs. Doomsday

What happens when Marvel’s darkest cosmic deity collides with DC’s most relentless biological engine of destruction?

On one side stands **Knull, the King in Black**—the primordial god of the abyss who ruled the void before the Big Bang, forged the All-Black Necrosword, decapitated Celestials, and ripped the Sentry in two.

On the other stands **Doomsday**—the prehistoric Kryptonian biological horror that beat Superman to death, broke Darkseid on Apokolips, and mutates in real time to survive any lethal blow.

This hypothetical cross-universe deathmatch tests the ultimate limits of **divine void sorcery versus unstoppable biological adaptation**.

---

## 1. Offensive Arsenals: God-Slayer vs. Bone Juggernaut

### Knull’s Lethal Arsenal:
* **All-Black the Necrosword:** The first symbiote ever forged, tempered in the divine blood of a murdered Celestial. The Necrosword bypasses conventional matter durability, severing immortal connections, slaying gods, and siphoning their life essence into the void.
* **Symbiote Hive-Mind Authority:** Knull can command billions of symbiote dragons, construct planet-sized shadow spheres, and infect celestial-tier minds with absolute mental subjugation.
* **Raw Physical Power:** In *King in Black* #1, Knull caught the charging Sentry (an entity with the power of a million exploding suns) and ripped him in half with his bare hands before absorbing the Void into himself.

### Doomsday’s Lethal Arsenal:
* **Reactive Evolution:** Whatever wounds or kills Doomsday triggers immediate cellular mutations that render him immune to that specific attack vector.
* **Infinite Physical Stamina:** Doomsday has no internal organs that tire, requires no oxygen or food, and can fight across eons without pause.
* **Apokoliptic Feats:** In *Hunter/Prey*, Doomsday invaded Apokolips, shrugged off Darkseid’s legendary **Omega Beams** (which normally disintegrate matter or erase targets from existence), and battered Darkseid into unconsciousness.

## 2. Round-by-Round Combat Simulation

### Phase 1: The Initial Impact
Doomsday leaps into combat with tectonic impact, swinging bone-plated fists capable of fracturing continental plates. Knull engages in hand-to-hand combat, utilizing his dark armor and immense physical strength. 
* Unlike mortal heroes, Knull does not feel physical pain from blunt trauma; his body is composed of primordial abyssal darkness.
* Doomsday’s initial punches will shatter Knull's jaw or crack his armor, but Knull regenerates instantaneously from the surrounding shadow.

### Phase 2: The Necrosword Strike
Recognizing Doomsday as an apex biological threat, Knull draws **All-Black the Necrosword**:
* When Knull strikes, the Necrosword will cleave clean through Doomsday’s calcium spikes and sever his limbs or decapitate the beast.
* In *Venom* #4, All-Black decapitated a full-grown Celestial whose armor withstood supernovas. Doomsday’s flesh cannot repel the divine edge of All-Black on the first strike.
* **Doomsday falls.**

### Phase 3: The Evolutionary Resurgence
Because Doomsday’s core genetic code cannot remain dead, his cellular structure violently regenerates:
* Doomsday’s body evolves dense anti-necrotic bone plating designed to absorb void-based cutting frequencies.
* He resurrects with heightened regenerative speed, attempting to crush Knull before the sword can strike again.

### Phase 4: The Symbiote Assimilation Crisis
Knull attempts to drown Doomsday in the black mass of the Hive:
* Tendrils of living symbiote enter Doomsday’s orifices, seeking to bind his neural pathways to Knull’s will.
* **The Backfire:** Doomsday’s biology is violently hostile to parasitic colonization. In comic history, Doomsday’s cellular structure produces toxic spores, lethal bio-acids, and hyper-kinetic shockwaves. Doomsday will violently incinerate the symbiote matter from within through reactive thermal mutation.

### Phase 5: The Endgame
While Doomsday can continually evolve to survive specific attacks, **he lacks the one weapon required to kill Knull: The Light.**
* Knull is an immortal concept of the void. He survived being imprisoned inside the planetary core of Klyntar for millions of years.
* Knull can only be destroyed by the **Enigma Force (Uni-Power / Captain Universe)**—the cosmic opposite of the abyss.
* Because Doomsday possesses no divine light, no magical radiance, and no cosmic consciousness, every punch Doomsday lands merely disperses shadows that reform moments later.
* Conversely, Knull can use the Necrosword to pin Doomsday, rip his soul/consciousness from his body, and seal his genetic remains into the center of an abyssal singularity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Could Doomsday eventually adapt to the All-Black Necrosword?
Doomsday can adapt to the physical cutting edge of the blade, developing denser armor. However, All-Black is not merely physical steel; it is a sentient mystical curse forged from the dark abyss that attacks the spiritual and existential matrix of the victim. Adapting to conceptual void necrosis requires reality-warping capability that Doomsday does not possess.

### What if Doomsday bonded with the Venom symbiote?
If Doomsday were bonded with a standard symbiote like Venom or Carnage, he would become an extinction-level catastrophe for both the Marvel and DC universes. However, against Knull, any standard symbiote remains subservient to the King in Black's psychic command.

### Did Knull really kill a Celestial?
Yes. In *Venom* (Vol. 4) #4, Knull forged the Necrosword from his own shadow inside the cosmic forge and leaped into the sky, severing the head of an armored Celestial in a single blow. The severed head floated through the cosmos and became the mining colony known as **Knowhere**.
