## The Battle Within: Who's Stronger, Void or Sentry?

Robert Reynolds does not simply possess a superhero alter-ego; he is host to two opposing cosmic extremes. The eternal question remains: **Who is stronger, Void or Sentry?**

### Why The Void Consistently Demonstrates Greater Strength:
1. **The Absence of Moral Restraint:** When operating as Sentry, Bob Reynolds is paralyzed by fear that unleashing his full solar potential will destroy the planet. The Void operates with zero ethical governor.
2. **Eldritch Matter Manipulation:** While Sentry typically relies on flight, energy blasts, and super strength, The Void freely alters reality, manifests darkness tendrils, and unleashes atmospheric corruption.
3. **The Merged Sentry Exception:** The only version stronger than both is the **Merged Sentry** (featured in *Sentry* 2018), where Bob harmonized the golden light and the shadow into a singular, balanced cosmic god.
