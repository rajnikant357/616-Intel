## Mechanics of Cosmic Dread: Why Is Void So Powerful?

To understand **why Void is so powerful**, one must understand that Robert Reynolds was never just a super-soldier. The **Golden Sentry Serum** shifted his bio-quantum frequency so that he became an anchor point for a cosmic duality.

### The Four Pillars of The Void's Power:
1. **Subatomic Reality Warping:** The Void does not just hit hard; it reorganizes matter. It can dissolve physical armor, turn air into razor-sharp black glass, and regenerate from complete atomic destruction.
2. **Psychic Terror Projection:** The Void emits an infrasonic empathic aura that paralyzes opponents with their deepest, most shameful fears, disabling heroes before physical contact occurs.
3. **Tendril Multiplication:** The Void can manifest millions of dark-matter tendrils simultaneously across thousands of miles, striking entire armies at once.
4. **Immortality:** As long as Sentry exists, The Void cannot be permanently extinguished.
