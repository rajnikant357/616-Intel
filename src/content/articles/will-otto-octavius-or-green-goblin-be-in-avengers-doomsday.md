## 🕷️ The *No Way Home* Aftermath: Where Did Doc Ock & Green Goblin Go?

To evaluate whether Otto Octavius or Norman Osborn can return in *Avengers: Doomsday*, we must first examine the canonical status of their timelines following *Spider-Man: No Way Home* (2021).

```
                 THE DIVERGENT TIMELINE DILEMMA
                 
       [ Original Raimi Timeline ]           [ "Cured" Branch Timelines ]
                  |                                        |
       Spider-Man 1: Osborn Impaled          Branched Earth: Osborn Survives
       Spider-Man 2: Octavius Drowns         Branched Earth: Octavius Cured
                  |                                        |
                  +--------------------+-------------------+
                                       |
                   [ MULTIVERSAL INCURSION COLLAPSE ]
                                       |
              Doom Draws Survived Timelines into Battleworld
```

1. **The Quantum Branching**: When Peter Parker (Tom Holland) developed neuro-cures for Norman Osborn’s goblin psychosis and Otto Octavius’s neural interface inhibitor chip, Doctor Strange sent them back to the exact moments before their tragic deaths.
2. **The Branch Paradox**: According to the MCU’s rules of temporal mechanics established in *Avengers: Endgame* and *Loki*, altering the past does not change the original future; it spawns divergent branching timelines. In one branch, a sane Norman Osborn returns to Oscorp; in another, Otto Octavius regains control of his robotic tentacles.
3. **The Incursion Threat**: As revealed in *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*, excessive dimensional travel destabilizes universal boundaries, triggering **Incursions**. The massive displacement caused by the *No Way Home* spell made Earth-616 and the Raimi universe (Earth-96283) prime candidates for catastrophic gravitational collision.
