## Investigating the King in Black: Will Knull Appear in Avengers: Doomsday?

Following his ominous live-action tease in *Venom: The Last Dance* (2024), Marvel fans around the globe have been asking: **Will there be Knull in Avengers: Doomsday?**

As the god of the symbiotes and creator of the All-Black Necrosword (wielded by Gorr the God Butcher in *Thor: Love and Thunder*), Knull represents one of the most terrifying primordial threats in Marvel Comics history. But does he fit into the multiversal incursion war orchestrated by Doctor Doom?

---

## 1. The Sony-Marvel Character Rights Equation

Knull’s cinematic rights are co-held between Sony Pictures and Marvel Studios as part of the broader Spider-Man character universe. Following the shared multiversal bridge established in *Spider-Man: No Way Home*, Sony and Marvel have established collaborative conduits for major crossover events.

Industry insiders note that while Knull is not the central antagonist of *Avengers: Doomsday* (a role firmly held by Robert Downey Jr.’s Doctor Doom), his dark realm of Klyntar could represent one of the fractured planetary fragments pulled into Battleworld for *Avengers: Secret Wars*.

---

## 2. Comic Precedent: Knull, Sentry, and Doctor Doom

In Marvel Comics canon, Knull has critical narrative links to both Sentry and Doctor Doom:
- **Knull Ripping Sentry in Half:** In *King in Black #1*, Knull executed one of the most violent feats in modern comics by seizing Sentry in mid-air and tearing him into two pieces, absorbing the dark essence of The Void into his symbiote horde.
- **Doom Resisting Knull:** In *King in Black: Doctor Doom*, Victor von Doom defended Latveria from symbiote infection using mystical counter-sigils, demonstrating that Doom's sorcery can neutralize Knull's corruptive black bio-matter.

---

## The Verdict on Knull in Doomsday
- **Direct Appearance in Doomsday:** Low to moderate probability (likely a post-credits tease or multiversal fragment cameo).
- **Appearance in Secret Wars / Battleworld:** High probability, as Battleworld canonically features a region known as the Symbiote Wasteland.
